<p>Weather shows the current conditions, the next 24 hours and a 7-day forecast for any place on Earth. Search for a city by name, or let the app use your phone's location. There is no account, no API key, no ads and no analytics.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Any place.</b> Start typing a city name to see matching places. Country and region are shown, so you can tell places with the same name apart.</li>
<li><b>Your location.</b> Tap "Use my current location" and the app shows the place name when it can, or the coordinates when it cannot. Location access is optional, and search works without it.</li>
<li><b>Saved places.</b> Keep several places and switch between them. They stay on your phone, and Android may include them in a backup of your phone.</li>
<li><b>The right local time.</b> Each place shows its own local time, and its weather icons show daytime or nighttime there. Tokyo shows Tokyo time, even when you are in Denmark.</li>
<li><b>Seven days to choose from.</b> Tap a day to see its weather summary, details and hour-by-hour forecast.</li>
<li><b>Home screen widget</b> for the place you selected. It refreshes about every half hour.</li>
<li><b>Metric or imperial.</b> Choose °C, m/s and mm, or °F, mph and inches.</li>
<li><b>Works without a connection.</b> If the forecast cannot be updated, the app shows the last forecast it saved for that place and says when that forecast is from.</li>
</ul>

<p>Forecasts and place search come from <a href="https://open-meteo.com">Open-Meteo</a>, used under their free non-commercial terms. Weather has no server of its own. When you use your location, your phone's own location and place-naming services help find and name the place.</p>
