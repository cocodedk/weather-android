      What it does

      See the current conditions, an hour-by-hour forecast for the next 24 hours and a seven-day forecast, for any place you choose.

      • Any location

        Start typing a city name to see matching places. Country and region are shown, so you can tell places with the same name apart.

      • Your location

        One tap finds your location and, when it can, the name of the place. Otherwise the app shows the coordinates. Permission is optional, and search keeps working if you say no.

      • The right local time

        Each place shows its own local time, with weather icons that show daytime or nighttime there, whatever time it is on your phone.

      • Seven days, selectable

        Tap a day to see its weather summary, details and hour-by-hour forecast.

      • Works without a connection

        If the forecast cannot be updated, the app shows the last forecast it saved for that place and says when that forecast is from.

      • Home screen widget

        Current conditions where you can see them without opening anything. It follows the location you picked in the app and taps through to the rest.

      • Nothing to sign up for

        No API key, no account, no analytics, no ads. Open-Meteo is free and needs no key, and there is no tracking in the app.

      Ported from a television

      This started as Copenhagen Weather, a Samsung Tizen TV app driven entirely by a remote control. It showed one hardcoded city, because a television sits in one room.

      The Android version keeps that app's colours, weather icons and way of choosing a day. It adds touch controls, and it lets you search for places or use your phone's location.

      Get it

      Android 8.0 or newer. The app is in English and Danish and follows your phone's language. The APK is signed and attached to every release.
